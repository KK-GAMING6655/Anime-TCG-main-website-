Bot Commands

Explore all available slash commands and their functions.

/gacha

Pull a single random card using your coins.

/bulk_gacha

Pull multiple cards at once to quickly expand your collection.

/daily

Claim your daily coin reward every 24 hours.

/beg

Ask for a small sum of free coins when you're broke.

/cointoss

Gamble your coins on a heads or tails coin flip.

/view_card

Inspect details, artwork, value, and owners of a specific card.

/card_list

Browse through all cards registered in the bot.

/card_leaderboard

View global player rankings based on total collection points.

/inventory

View and scroll through your personal card collection.

/balance

Check your current coin balance.

/user_inventory

Inspect another member's card collection (if public).

/user_balance

Check another member's coin balance (if public).

/login

Generate your Web Portal credentials to log in on the website.

/burn

Convert duplicate cards into coins instantly.

/market

Browse cards currently listed for sale on the global market.

/market_sell

Put one of your cards up for sale on the global market.

/market_remove

Cancel your card listing and retrieve it from the market.

/rarity_list

Check all card rarities, custom colors, and drop chances.

/help

Open the interactive help menu with guides and details.
